Bewotec

Designing for complex travel workflows

A professional booking workspace for travel agents handling complex, information-heavy journeys. The challenge was not to remove complexity, but to make it clearer and easier to work with every day.

Role
UX Designer / Design Consultant
Context
While working at Vaadin, I joined the Bewotec project as a design consultant, helping shape the experience for a specialist travel product built around demanding professional workflows.
Focus
Enterprise UXComplex workflowsData-heavy UIVaadin
The product

A toolbox for travel agents

Travel agents can work with large amounts of information while building complex bookings. Bewotec needed an interface that could support that density without getting in the way of professional users.

The work focused on clarity, efficiency and components tailored to the needs of the travel workflow.

The result

Complex information, kept usable

The resulting product was designed for professional use: information-rich, data-intensive and supported by highly tailored components while maintaining clarity for the people using it every day.
